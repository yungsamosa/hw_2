Both schemas use the same six tables and the same relationships, so they model AttendanceChimp identically at a structural level. The second schema is simpler and easier to implement, since it includes only the attributes the assignment requires. The first schema is more complete, adding expiring QR tokens, image hashing, and device and IP tracking to resist cheating. It also includes a unique constraint on enrollments and separate start and end times, which make attendance analytics more reliable. That extra detail comes at the cost of more fields to build, validate, and maintain. A practical middle ground would start from the simpler schema and add only the most important anti-cheating fields, like the QR expiry and image hash.
